# DIGITAL AI SUPPLIER — Brand Identity

## Confirmed brand name
DIGITAL AI SUPPLIER

This is the primary product/company brand name confirmed by the owner. Do not use “AI Seller Hub” as the main brand name in new screens, metadata, login pages, dashboard headers or customer-facing material.

## Required brand placement
- Prominent logo and wordmark on landing page and main dashboard.
- Logo on login, registration, OTP verification and password recovery screens.
- Compact brand mark in navigation and mobile layouts.
- Consistent brand identity in seller storefront administration and customer-facing pages, while allowing each seller's own store branding.
- Brand name on help, notifications, reports and generated documents where appropriate.
- Responsive logo sizing and accessible alt text.

## Logo asset
`public/digital-ai-supplier-logo.svg` is the initial scalable vector logo concept. It uses a connected-store/network motif and the DIGITAL AI SUPPLIER wordmark. It is a starter design and should be reviewed by the owner before final trademark/marketing use.

## Design principles
Welcoming, trustworthy, modern, accessible, multilingual, readable on mobile and desktop. Do not imply government endorsement or marketplace affiliation.
